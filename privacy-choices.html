Your privacy choices

Last updated: 10 November 2026

NixVisuals for Safari processes webpages in your browser. The developer does not receive your browsing history, site lists, or saved media choices. There is no NixVisuals account, advertising tracking, or sale of personal data to opt out of.

Choose which websites it can access

Open Safari’s extension settings, select NixVisuals, and review its website permissions. Allow access only to websites you choose, revoke access, or turn off the extension. Private Browsing access is a separate Safari permission.

Change or delete your saved choices

Open NixVisuals from Safari’s extensions menu and choose Settings. You can edit your block and allow lists and remove permanent image or video choices. To clear all saved site lists and permanent media choices and restore the initial settings, choose Reset to defaults and confirm. Resetting restores the default behavior; turn off the extension separately if you want it to stop running.

Temporary per-page choices end when you reload, navigate away, or close the tab, depending on the choice. Permanent image and video choices are saved on that device.

Apple sync and exported files

Safari may sync supported settings through your Apple account when Safari iCloud sync is enabled. Review Safari’s iCloud settings on your Apple devices to manage that sync. The developer cannot access or delete data in your Apple account. Reset settings on the devices you use and check other synced devices before uninstalling. Delete any exported settings files, and copies or backups of those files, separately wherever you saved them.

Stop using NixVisuals

You can disable NixVisuals in Safari’s extension settings or uninstall the app. If you also want to clear saved choices, use Reset to defaults first. Uninstalling alone may not clear synced copies or backups.

Ask a privacy question

Email sebproductionsmail@gmail.com for help with privacy choices or to request deletion of support emails you previously sent. There is no developer-held browsing profile to retrieve or delete. Do not send passwords or sensitive browsing information.
