NixVisuals support

NixVisuals helps you control images and videos on websites in Safari on Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Contact us

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, email sebproductionsmail@gmail.com.

Include your device type, operating system version, NixVisuals version, and a description of what happened. A website address is optional if needed to reproduce an issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive browsing information. If you attach screenshots, remove private information first.

Enable NixVisuals

Open Safari’s extension settings, turn on NixVisuals, and allow access to the websites where you want it to work. On iPhone and iPad, you can also find Safari’s extension controls in the Settings app under Apps → Safari → Extensions. On Mac, use Safari → Settings → Extensions.

Change what gets hidden

Open NixVisuals from Safari’s extensions menu. Use its controls for the current website, or choose Settings to edit your site lists and display preferences. Check Safari website permissions if the extension has no effect.

Reset settings or manage privacy

In NixVisuals Settings, choose Reset to defaults to clear site lists and permanent media choices. See your privacy choices for clearing data, managing permissions, and Apple sync. Read our privacy policy for details about data handling.
